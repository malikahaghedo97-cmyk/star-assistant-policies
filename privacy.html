Star Assistant — Privacy Policy Last Updated: September 2026 1. About This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy explains how Star Assistant, a Discord bot operated by XMALI, collects, uses, and stores information when you interact with the bot or use features provided by it. Star Assistant operates through Discord and may process information made available to it by Discord or provided through features that you choose to use. 2. Information Star Assistant May Collect Depending on the features being used, Star Assistant may store information associated with your Discord account or your activity within a server using the bot. This may include: * Discord user ID * Discord server ID * Discord usernames or display information * Roles and permissions relevant to bot features * Information required for moderation and server-management features * XP, levels, and other progression information * Game, collection, evolution, or reward information * Information required for tracking features * Verification information generated during the verification process * Information contained in moderation records or staff logs * Ticket or transcript information where the server uses Star Assistant’s ticket features The exact information stored depends on which Star Assistant features are enabled or used by a particular server. 3. Verification Information Blessings Zone may use Star Assistant’s verification system. When you use the verification system, Star Assistant may process information required to complete the Discord OAuth verification process and generate security signals used to help identify potentially suspicious or duplicate accounts. Some verification information is stored in a protected form rather than as the original value. Verification information is used to: * Complete the verification process * Help identify potentially suspicious verification attempts * Determine whether an account can be automatically verified * Send certain cases for staff review * Maintain verification records and logs Star Assistant does not treat a verification signal as definitive proof that a user is an alternate account. Verification results may instead be used as part of an automated decision or staff review. Verification is a feature specific to Blessings Zone and is not used as a verification system for Kirby Stars. 4. How Information Is Used Information processed by Star Assistant may be used to: * Provide bot features * Maintain user progression and game data * Provide moderation and server-management functionality * Operate verification and security features * Maintain logs and records for server staff * Prevent abuse or misuse of bot features * Troubleshoot technical problems * Maintain and improve the reliability of Star Assistant Star Assistant does not use collected information to sell personal data to advertisers. 5. Discord Information Star Assistant is a third-party application operating on Discord. Discord independently processes information in accordance with Discord’s own Privacy Policy. Star Assistant does not control Discord’s collection or processing of information outside of the information made available to the bot through Discord and the features Star Assistant provides. You can review Discord’s Privacy Policy on Discord’s website. 6. Information Sharing Star Assistant does not sell personal information. Information may be visible to authorised server administrators or moderators where that information is part of a server feature, moderation record, verification result, log, ticket, or other server-management function. Information may also be processed by hosting or infrastructure services used to operate Star Assistant where necessary to provide the bot’s functionality. 7. Data Retention Information may be retained for as long as it is required to provide the relevant Star Assistant feature, maintain server functionality, preserve necessary moderation or security records, or otherwise fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. Retention may differ depending on the type of information and the feature involved. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or otherwise removed from Star Assistant’s active records. 8. Verification Privacy Requests Users may request information relating to their Star Assistant verification data or request deletion of verification information where the available Star Assistant privacy controls permit this. Star Assistant also provides the /verification_privacy command for applicable verification-related privacy requests. For additional questions or requests concerning verification data, contact @xmali8627 on Discord. 9. Data Security Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Star Assistant from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. 10. Your Choices You can choose whether to interact with Star Assistant’s features. Server administrators also control whether Star Assistant is installed in their server and which features are enabled. Where Star Assistant provides a specific privacy or deletion control, users may use that feature to manage the applicable information. 11. Children’s Privacy Star Assistant operates through Discord. Users must meet Discord’s applicable minimum age requirements to use Discord. Star Assistant is not intended to knowingly collect information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord under Discord’s applicable age requirements. Discord’s own age requirements and policies apply to use of the Discord platform. (Discord) 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time when Star Assistant’s features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available through the Star Assistant website. 13.
